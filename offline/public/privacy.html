Zeta Co-Pilot privacy policy

Effective 15 October 2026

This policy is for Zeta Co-Pilot only. The published page is https://www.cloudastick.com/zeta-co-pilot-privacy.

Which app

Zeta Co-Pilot, package com.zetapharma.fieldpwa, is for Zeta Pharma field teams. Cloudastick Systems, a Salesforce Partner, provides the app. People sign in with their company Salesforce account. Zeta Co-Pilot is not a Salesforce product.

Who it is for

Company users, such as representatives and managers. It is not for children.

What it handles

Data the app handles, why, and who receives it
Data Why Shared with
Salesforce sign-in session, kept on the device Stay signed in The user’s Salesforce org, and Cloudastick, which hosts the app at salesforceoffline.com and passes sign-in and data requests
Work records the user can already see: accounts, visits, meetings, and related records. A copy can stay on the device for offline work, then sync back Do the job online and offline Zeta Pharma’s Salesforce org
Camera, only while the meeting scanner is open Scan a meeting code Not stored as a photo library
Microphone, and a recording or transcript only when the user allows a call Place or record a call. Transcription runs on the device The company Salesforce org if the call result is saved there
Notification permission Deliver alerts the user allowed Google, for delivery
Online or offline status Know whether to sync Not sold or used for ads

What it does not do

No ads. Personal information is not sold.

How long it stays

The session and offline copy remain on the device until logout or uninstall. Records in Salesforce follow Zeta Pharma’s own retention rules.

Choices

Camera, microphone, and notifications can be turned off in device settings. Those features then stop. Removing the local copy means log out and uninstall.

Contact

Cloudastick Systems, ArdelGolf, Nasr City, Cairo. support@cloudastick.com. Questions about a person’s work records go to the Zeta Pharma administrator.
