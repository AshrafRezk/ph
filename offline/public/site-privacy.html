Privacy policy

Effective 19 October 2026

This policy is for the shared website at salesforceoffline.com. Cloudastick Systems, a Salesforce Partner, provides the site. It is not a Salesforce product, and it is not branded for one company until someone signs in.

Who it is for

Company users, such as representatives and managers. It is not for children.

What it handles

You sign in to your company’s Salesforce org. A sign-in session and an offline copy of the records you use stay on this device so work can continue without internet. Those records live in your company’s Salesforce org. How long the company keeps them follows that company’s own rules.

After you sign in, the site can show your company’s name and logo. A downloaded Android or iOS app may be branded for one company from the start. That app has its own privacy policy.

What it does not do

No ads. Personal information is not sold.

How long it stays

The session and offline copy remain on the device until logout or until they are removed.
